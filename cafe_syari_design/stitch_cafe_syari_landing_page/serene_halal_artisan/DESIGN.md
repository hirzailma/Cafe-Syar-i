## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene, hospitable, and mindful atmosphere of an artisan halal coffeehouse. Bridging contemporary Indonesian cafe culture with minimalist Islamic principles of modesty, purity (*thayyib*), and warmth, the aesthetic relies on architectural clarity, generous breathing room, and artisanal textures.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Warmth**:
- **Purity & Serenity:** Uncluttered layouts, calm compositional balance, and natural tonal harmony evoke mindful tranquility.
- **Artisan Hospitality:** Grounded espresso hues and soothing herbal greens establish an organic, ethical connection to single-origin roasts and halal botanicals.
- **Modest Refinement:** Avoiding flashy micro-animations or hyper-saturated gradients, the interface elevates typographic nuance, subtle organic borders, and soft daylight contrasts.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the sensory journey of halal coffee craft—from steamed oat milk and raw honey to sun-dried coffee leaves and dark roasted beans.

- **Primary (`#4C4541` - Roasted Espresso):** Used for primary actions, structural titles, primary icons, and high-emphasis body text. Grounded and authoritative without the sterile starkness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#AEAC78` - Muted Sage Olive):** Captures the natural, halal, and botanical character. Applied to active states, ethical certifications, badges, and secondary interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F2C46A` - Warm Honey Gold):** Symbolizes artisan excellence, warmth, and hospitality. Reserved for feature highlights, rating stars, seasonal specials, and conversion prompts.
- **Neutral (`#FCF0DA` - Warm Cream Canvas):** The foundational surface layer. Provides an inviting parchment-like warmth far softer than standard sterile displays.
- **Canvas White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated cards, active input fields, and foreground content cards to create clean architectural layering.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve contemporary Indonesian design excellence with balanced warmth and high legibility.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and bold weights to project modern artisanal confidence.
- **Editorial Sub-headers:** Utilize medium weights paired with generous line-heights, ensuring coffee origin stories, menu tasting notes, and halal sourcing declarations read effortlessly.
- **Body & Labels:** Emphasize open apertures and high x-height for clear legibility on mobile ordering screens, physical QR menus, and product packaging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a relaxed, mindful rhythm with purposeful white space to emulate an open, light-filled cafe interior.

- **Grid Strategy:** A responsive 12-column layout on desktop (max container width 1200px), transforming to a 6-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Safe Margins:** Generous vertical sections prevent cognitive fatigue. Canvas margins compress gracefully from `2rem` on desktop down to `1rem` on mobile.
- **Component Breathing Space:** Menu listings, filter chips, and card grids use consistent increments of `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) to retain airiness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **Tonal Layering** paired with **Warm Ambient Diffusions**, avoiding heavy synthetic dropshadows.

- **Base Layer:** The global canvas rests on `#FCF0DA` (Warm Cream).
- **Surface Elevation:** Interactive containers, cards, and dialogue modals sit on `#FFFFFF`, creating natural separation without requiring dark structural borders.
- **Ambient Lighting:** Shadows are soft, low-opacity, and warm-tinted (`rgba(76, 69, 65, 0.06)` with an 8px–20px blur radius). This evokes the gentle diffusion of morning cafe sunlight.
- **Hairline Framing:** Subtle structural divisions rely on semi-transparent coffee borders (`rgba(76, 69, 65, 0.12)`) rather than harsh dividers.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (level `2`), giving components a tactile, crafted feel without appearing overly toy-like.

- **Standard Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, secondary buttons, notification banners, and item thumbnails.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Used for primary menu cards, order drawers, and specialty spotlight panels.
- **Pill Radius (Full / 9999px):** Dedicated strictly to chips, status tags (e.g., "100% Halal Certified", "Single Origin"), and quick-action floaters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#4C4541` (Roasted Espresso) background with `#FCF0DA` text, subtle 8px radius, and standard padding of `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover scales lightly with 90% opacity.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined button with 1px border of `#AEAC78` (Muted Sage), sage text, and transparent surface; fills to 10% sage tint on hover.
- **Tertiary / Specialty:** Accent background of `#F2C46A` (Warm Honey Gold) with `#4C4541` dark text, reserved for "Order Now" or seasonal signature beverages.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Product Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-lg` (16px), bordered with a faint 1px hairline (`rgba(76, 69, 65, 0.08)`). Displays drink title in `headline-sm`, tasting notes in `body-sm`, and price tagged cleanly in espresso brown.
- **Story / Cert Card:** Soft sage-tinted card (`#AEAC78` at 12% fill) with muted sage text and gold iconography detailing halal roasting compliance and bean origins.

### Chips & Filters
- **Dietary & Flavor Filters:** Fully rounded pill shapes. Inactive state features `#FCF0DA` fill with `#4C4541` typography. Active state switches to `#AEAC78` with crisp white typography.

### Input Fields
- **Fields & Search:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface resting on the `#FCF0DA` canvas with 8px radius, 1px border in `rgba(76, 69, 65, 0.2)`. Focus transitions to a 1.5px solid border in `#AEAC78` with a soft sage glow.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded corners on checkboxes (4px) and pure circles for radios. Inactive border of `#4C4541` (muted); active states fill with `#4C4541` and `#FFFFFF` glyph checks.

### Halal & Artisan Badges
- **Trust Elements:** Elegant, compact badges featuring delicate geometric line motifs inspired by Islamic architectural symmetry, executed strictly in monochrome `#4C4541` or paired with `#AEAC78`.
